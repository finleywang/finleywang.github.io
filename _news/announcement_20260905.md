AI Plus \|  Prof. Fei Wang will serve as Executive Editor-in-Chief for a new international, peer-reviewed journal, entitled ["AI Plus"](https://www.the-innovation.org/ai-plus)!

---

AI Plus is an international, peer-reviewed journal dedicated to publishing high-quality research in artificial intelligence and its interdisciplinary applications.

By fostering a collaborative space that is multi-role-participatory, problem-driven, and continuously evolving, we seek to advance interdisciplinary innovation in AI and promote AI for engineering, industry, and society.
